# ERF-51 normalization conformance cases.
#
# Format, after the model of Unicode's NormalizationTest.txt: one case per
# line, RAW <TAB> EXPECTED. Blank lines and lines opening with # are ignored.
# \n in either column means a newline, \t a tab; nothing else is escaped.
#
# The sequence is six unwrapping steps (a-f) followed by ten normalization
# steps (1-10), applied identically to a quote and to a capture. Case is
# never folded.
#
# A failing line is a finding about the implementation, not a licence to
# edit the expectation. See README.md.

# --- a. markdown link syntax reduces to its link text ---------------------
The [reported total](https://ex.test/data) was 17.	The reported total was 17.
[a](b) and [c](d)	a and c
[](https://ex.test/x) empty text	empty text

# --- b. attribute blobs in braces are removed ------------------------------
heading {#anchor .cls} text	heading text
a {} b	a b

# --- c. parenthesized link targets are removed -----------------------------
see (https://ex.test/x) here	see here
protocol relative (//ex.test/y) here	protocol relative here
fragment (#section-2) here	fragment here
root relative (/docs/page) here	root relative here

# --- d. blockquote markers at line start, with one following space ---------
> quoted line	quoted line
>no space after marker	no space after marker
line one\n> line two	line one line two

# --- e. brackets, straight double quotes, and symbols ----------------------
[bracketed]	bracketed
"straight quoted"	straight quoted
Acme(R) and (TM) and (C)	Acme(R) and (TM) and (C)
caret ^ and backslash \	caret and backslash

# --- f. a space before punctuation is an export artifact -------------------
word , next	word, next
end of sentence .	end of sentence.
colon : here	colon: here

# --- 1. Unicode NFKC --------------------------------------------------------
ﬁnal ligature	final ligature
①	1

# --- 2. soft hyphens --------------------------------------------------------
soft­hyphen	softhyphen

# --- 3. typographic single quotes fold to ' --------------------------------
it’s a quote	it's a quote
‘single’	'single'

# --- 4. typographic double quotes fold to " --------------------------------
# NOTE: step e removes the STRAIGHT double quote and runs BEFORE step 4, so a
# curly pair survives e and is then folded into straight quotes that nothing
# removes. Two spellings of one quotation therefore normalize differently.
# This case records the implementation's actual behaviour so the divergence
# is visible rather than assumed; see the finding in the suite report.
“curly”	"curly"

# --- 5. dash variants fold to - ---------------------------------------------
en–dash	en-dash
em—dash	em-dash
minus−sign	minus-sign

# --- 6. words broken across lines ------------------------------------------
inter-\nnational	international
hyphen-\n   indented	hyphenindented

# --- 7. runs of two or more hyphens collapse to one ------------------------
a--b	a-b
a-----b	a-b

# --- 8. emphasis and code markers are removed ------------------------------
*bold* and _under_ and `code`	bold and under and code

# --- 9. dash spacing is unified --------------------------------------------
a - b	a-b
a  -  b	a-b

# --- 10. whitespace runs collapse, then trim -------------------------------
  lots   of    space  	lots of space
line\nbreak	line break

# --- case MUST NOT be folded ------------------------------------------------
The Study Found	The Study Found
MiXeD CaSe	MiXeD CaSe

# --- combinations, closer to real capture text ------------------------------
> The [study](https://ex.test/s) found *no* effect .	The study found no effect.
“Nested [link](https://ex.test/l) inside a quote”	"Nested link inside a quote"
