# What this format is, and is not

A specification that says only what it does leaves its reader to discover
its edges by hitting them. This document states both halves, and names who
owns the ground it declines.

## What it does

**Captures evidence that stays checkable.** A quote is recorded verbatim
against a frozen copy of its source, and the match is a mechanical check
anyone holding both can re-run years later. Provenance, not memory, is what
survives. The archival tradition (diplomatics, InterPARES) has judged
records this way since 1681; what is new here is the unit being a passage
rather than a document, small enough to check by machine.

**Types claims by what would check them.** A claim declares whether data
settles it, reasoning settles it, the world will settle it, or the author's
own conduct settles it. The type is the backing contract, so what counts as
support is decided when the claim is written rather than argued afterwards.

**Records searches as evidence.** A survey records what was sought, with
which instrument, and what came back. This is what lets a claim about
absence rest on something citable, where otherwise absence rests on an
impression. Nothing else in the surveyed field models the search itself as
a record.

**Keeps a standings ledger.** Who stands behind a claim, since when, and
why, append-only, per person, humans only. A position is never a status
field someone overwrites; it is a dated entry with a reason, and the claim's
reading is computed from the entries rather than stored. This and the
evidence primitive are the two elements the prior-art survey found nowhere
else.

**Binds prose to claims.** A narrative is written for people, and each
passage that asserts something names the claims it rests on. When the
thinking underneath changes, the prose that depended on it can be found.

## What it does not do

Each of these is a deliberate boundary, not an omission awaiting repair.
Where a field already owns the ground, it is named.

**Full argumentation theory.** The format has an argument kind, typed
relations, and premise closures, but it is a thin layer on purpose: no
warrants, no argument schemes, no typed defeaters, four relations where
CiTO has forty. *Owned by:* Toulmin, Walton, the AIF and Carneades
tradition. *Why thin:* the surveyed evidence is that small vocabularies get
used and large ones get skipped.

**Decisions and outcomes.** What was *done* about a claim is out of scope.
*Owned by:* the architecture-decision-record tradition (Nygard, MADR).
*Why:* an active bet plus the standing that activated it covers the solo
case, and a neighbouring system can consume these records.

**Conflict resolution.** The format records that two claims are in tension
and that people disagree. It supplies no tie-break and no procedure for
settling either. Contested is a terminal reading, not a state resolved by
arithmetic. *Owned by:* deliberation and issue-based systems (IBIS and
descendants).

**Confidentiality enforcement.** A corpus may carry a classification label
the format records and never reads. Which corpora may cite or travel with
which is deployment policy. *Why:* this version specifies no policies at
all, and a rule about who may see what is a policy wearing a schema.

**Degrees of belief.** No probabilities, no confidence scores, no strength
grades. *Why:* they invite false precision and averaging over judgments
that were never commensurable. Settledness is readable from tenure,
disputes survived, and evidence accumulated.

**Multi-party collaboration.** One human per corpus is the practice the
format was extracted from. Actor registries, import provenance, and
identifier collision between two writers are all deferred behind the arrival
of a second human. *Owned for now by:* nothing here; see `backlog.md`.

**Ethics, beyond first-person commitment.** The `commitment` kind records
conduct its author has chosen and will be held to, which is a deontic
statement about oneself. Obligation to others, permission structures, and
any ethical framework are absent entirely. *Owned by:* normative ethics,
and no format should pretend otherwise.

**Interpretation.** Whether an excerpt fairly represents its source,
whether a finding overstates its quote, whether a title still says what its
body says: all judgments, all attributed to a named actor with a date, none
mechanized. The format's discipline is to make the judgment visible and
name who made it, never to automate it away.

## The shape of the boundary

Read together, the boundary has one logic. **The format records what was
found, what is asserted, what was searched, and who stands behind it. Every
question of what to *do* about any of that belongs to whoever runs the
corpus.** That is why there are no gates, no policies, no thresholds and no
resolution procedures: not because those questions are unimportant, but
because they are answered per use, and a format that answered them once
would be wrong everywhere else.
