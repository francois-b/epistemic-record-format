## Working notes

The IEA projects global data-centre electricity consumption will keep rising past its 2030 forecast to around 1,200 TWh by 2035, and separately forecasts US electricity demand growing about 2% annually through 2027, an upward revision it attributes largely to data-centre consumption.

The IEA's April 2025 Energy and AI report projects that, in its Base Case, global data-centre electricity consumption will rise to around 1,200 TWh by 2035, beyond its own 2030 projection of around 945 TWh (acx-83); the IEA's February 2025 Electricity 2025 report forecasts US electricity demand growing at an average annual rate of 2% over 2025-2027, equivalent to adding California's total electricity demand over three years, an upward revision from its January 2024 forecast driven largely by higher data-centre consumption (acx-84).
