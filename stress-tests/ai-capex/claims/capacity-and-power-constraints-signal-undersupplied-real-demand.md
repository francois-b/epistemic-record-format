## Working notes

Data-center vacancy rates, PJM capacity-auction shortfalls, memory-chip capacity constraints, and AWS's own reported unserved demand together indicate AI infrastructure demand currently exceeds available supply in several markets.

North American data-center vacancy has held at 1% for a third consecutive year despite 66+ GW under construction (acx-104); PJM's 2028/2029 capacity auction cleared at the FERC-approved price cap and still fell 6,831 MW short of the reliability requirement, the first RTO-wide shortfall in PJM's history alongside the prior auction (acx-102, acx-103); AWS reports capacity constraints yielding unserved customer demand even after adding 3.9 GW in 2025 (acx-10); Microsoft reports an $80B backlog of unfulfillable Azure orders attributed to power constraints (acx-139); Samsung and SK hynix both report record results with memory (DRAM/HBM) capacity described as limited relative to AI server demand (acx-92, acx-93, acx-127). atoms_against carries the two atoms that complicate a pure supply-shortage reading at the level of actually-drawn electricity: Dominion's own 47 GW of contracted/pending capacity sits well above its own 16.6 GW demand forecast for 2046 (acx-100), and ERCOT's approved-to-energize large loads show observed peak draw well below the approved MW figure (acx-101). Those two atoms are the evidentiary core of reported-contracted-capacity-figures-overstate-realized-electricity-demand, with which this claim carries a conflicts-with edge.
