## Working notes

Reported cloud revenue and operating income growth at Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon in 2026 remained strong and, per company statements, increasingly broad-based beyond frontier AI labs, even as that growth was accompanied by declining or negative free cash flow at some of the same companies.

Microsoft's FY2026 Cloud revenue surpassed $214B with ~90% from non-frontier-model customers (acx-62); Google Cloud grew revenue 82% with backlog reaching $514B (acx-70); AWS operating income rose to $16.6B from $10.2B YoY (acx-67); Alphabet's Q2 2026 capex of $44.9B ran alongside negative $5.9B free cash flow for the quarter (acx-31, included here for the 'accompanied by' half of the title, not as revenue evidence). The claim is deliberately double-sided in its own title -- strong revenue AND deteriorating cash flow, both true at the same companies -- rather than picking one framing. atoms_against is used here to flag the specific counter-read that complicates 'broadening': acx-22 (the OpenAI-discount concentration finding, which cuts against 'broad-based' for Microsoft's AI-specific revenue even while its overall cloud revenue is genuinely broad), and Amazon's and Oracle's negative/shrinking free cash flow (acx-11, acx-8) as the companies where 'strong revenue growth' has not translated to cash generation this period.
