## What I was after

Whether any primary document -- a Buffon-authored passage, a contemporaneous
letter, a press notice -- shows Buffon actually retracting or revising the
American degeneracy thesis after receiving Jefferson's moose shipment in
October 1787, as opposed to the popular retelling (which this corpus's
narrative also leans on) that he merely promised to and then died first.

## What surprised me

How cleanly the popular "he promised, then died" story survives contact with
a search: every secondary account tells the same version, which makes it
look well-sourced by repetition -- but the one time a specific quotation
("promised in his next volume to set these things right") could be chased to
an origin, the origin turned out to be Jefferson's own memory, aired to
Daniel Webster around 1824, not a document from the 1787-88 window. That is
a fact about the story's evidentiary shape (a widely repeated claim resting
on one late, secondhand recollection) that is easy to miss when the story is
told as settled history.

## What I'd search differently next time

These four searches all ran against a general web index (via the Claude
Code WebSearch tool), which favors popular secondary retellings of a famous
episode over primary bibliographic detail. A next pass would run directly
against a full-text index of the Histoire Naturelle's own posthumous
volumes (Buffon died in 1788, but Lacépède and others continued publishing
under the Buffon name into the 1780s-1800s) and against Jefferson's own
later correspondence and the 1787 Paris press, rather than against a
general web index tuned for the popular-history version of the anecdote.

## Coverage bounds

This survey does not license reading the American-web-index absence as
proof that no primary Buffon retraction exists: the search universe was a
general web index, not the Histoire Naturelle's own later volumes or 18th-
century French periodicals, and each search's yield was inspected only to
the depth of its top-listed results (the search tool's own reported set),
not by opening and reading every linked page. Claim
`bjd-no-primary-retraction-confirmed` cites this survey on that scoped
basis, per ERF-25.
