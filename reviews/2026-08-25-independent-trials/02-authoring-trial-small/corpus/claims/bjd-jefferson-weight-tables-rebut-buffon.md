Jefferson's Notes on the State of Virginia rebuts Buffon's degeneracy
thesis on its own evidentiary terms, using the mammoth's size, a
comparative weight table, and named domestic-animal weights to argue
that New World quadrupeds are not, as a class, smaller than their Old
World counterparts.

## Working notes

Jefferson does not simply assert the opposite of Buffon; he takes
Buffon's own comparative, weight-based method and re-runs it (Query VI
states outright that he takes Buffon "for my ground work"). Four atoms
carry different pieces of that method: bjd-004 (the mammoth's sheer
size, on Buffon's own admission), bjd-005 (Jefferson's own summary
argument from the second table -- the single largest American species
alone outweighs the combined European column), bjd-006 (concrete,
named bullock weights offered as field evidence for domestic cattle),
and bjd-007 (a specific row from the first table -- American bear at
three times the European bear's listed weight).

This claim is deliberately scoped to the *size/diminution* strand of
the rebuttal. Jefferson's Query VI also argues at length against
Buffon's separate claims about Native Americans, in a register this
corpus's four sources touch (bjd's Buffon-quotation atoms do not extend
there) but do not evidence with atoms; that argument is out of scope
for this claim and this corpus.
