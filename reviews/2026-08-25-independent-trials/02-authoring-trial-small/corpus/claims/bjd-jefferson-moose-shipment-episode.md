  ## Working notes

  bjd-008 is the presentation itself, in Jefferson's own words to Buffon:
  moose bones and skin, a second moose's horns, and horns of caribou,
  elk, deer, spiked-horned buck, and roebuck, all from New Hampshire and
  Massachusetts.

  bjd-009 is worth keeping attached to this claim rather than treated as
  a footnote: in the very same letter, Jefferson volunteers that the elk
  horns sent are "remarkeably small" and that he has personally seen elk
  horns five or six times heavier. That is Jefferson holding his own
  specimen to the standard he is about to hold Buffon's argument to --
  he does not let an unrepresentative sample stand uncaveated, even one
  that serves his own case. It also means this claim should not be read
  as "Jefferson proved American animals are big by sending big horns";
  the horns he sent were, by his own word, on the small side, and the
  moose itself was the point of the shipment, not the elk horns.

  No `atoms_against` is recorded for this claim; the moose shipment as an
  event is not in dispute across the sources this corpus captured.
