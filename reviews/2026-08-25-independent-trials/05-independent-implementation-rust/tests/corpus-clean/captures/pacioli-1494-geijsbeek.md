# Particularis de Computis et Scripturis — chapter 36 (Geijsbeek translation, 1914)

*Excerpt. The surrounding paragraphs are retained so the quoted passage's place in the
chapter is legible.*

At the beginning of the ledger you shall write the trial balance, and thereafter every
transaction shall be entered as the memorandum directs.

All entries made in the led-
ger have to be double entries — that is, if you make one creditor, you must make some one
debtor. This is the rule that the merchant of Venice keeps above all others, and the
bookkeeper who departs from it will not find his ledger in agreement at the year's end.

Of the trial balance and of its use in proving the ledger, we shall say more in the
chapters that follow.
