Learning played no big role in the 1997 program that first beat a world chess champion.

## Working notes

Mostly true, with a caveat the builders themselves supply: a comparison-training tool learned pawn-shelter weights and showed the hand-tuned values were systematically too low, and those weights were raised before the match. A tertiary source goes further and says the evaluation parameters were set by analyzing thousands of master games, which the excerpt of the builders' paper held here does not corroborate. 'No big role' survives; 'no role' would not.
