## Working notes

A universal, stated with 'always', and therefore refutable by a single documented case where a built-in prior hurt from the start. The searching did not find one stated in those terms; what it found was a large literature arguing the opposite, that a well-matched inductive bias is necessary for generalization at all. Absence of a counterexample in a web search is weak evidence and the survey says so.
