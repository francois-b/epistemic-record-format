## Working notes

True over the decade, contestable over the competition. Two atoms on the against side deserve attention: hidden Markov models are described in the same encyclopedia entry as the thing that let researchers combine knowledge sources in one probabilistic model, which is a framework for organising human knowledge rather than for discarding it; and the Jelinek anecdote everyone quotes for this claim comes with its own source saying the wording and dating are unclear.
