Chess researchers who had pursued human-knowledge methods responded that brute-force search was not a general strategy and not how people play chess.

## Working notes

This one backs cleanly, and from a better source than the essay offers. McCarthy states both halves of the objection in his own name: that the programs solved his test position by brute force, and that humans use a much simpler process that does not involve brute force. He adds a 1997 note applying the charge to Deep Blue by name. The essay attributes the words to nobody; the corpus can now name a speaker.
