In the long run, knowledge built into an agent plateaus and even inhibits further progress.

## Working notes

The AlphaGo Zero authors state the mechanism precisely, that expert data can impose a ceiling, and then demonstrate it by removing the data and winning a hundred to nil. That is the strongest single piece of evidence in the essay's favour anywhere in this corpus.

Against it sits AlphaFold, whose authors attribute their result to architectures and training procedures built on the evolutionary, physical and geometric constraints of proteins, and which still ends with a hand-built force field doing the final refinement. Two named practitioners quoted by Nielsen say the same about lattice QCD and molecular dynamics: baking the symmetry in is what makes the problem tractable at all.

The two sides may both be right about different domains, which is what Rezende says explicitly. The essay does not admit that reading.
