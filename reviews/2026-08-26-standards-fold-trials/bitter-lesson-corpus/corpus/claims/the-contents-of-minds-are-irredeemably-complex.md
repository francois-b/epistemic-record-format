The actual contents of minds are tremendously and irredeemably complex, so simple ways of thinking about space, objects, agents or symmetries will not capture them.

## Working notes

A bet on how the world turns out, stated as a conclusion. Goyal and Bengio open their paper with the opposite hypothesis in as many words, that human and animal intelligence might be explained by a few principles rather than an encyclopedic list of heuristics, and treat that hypothesis as the thing worth working on. Symmetry is on the essay's list of things not to build in and is precisely what Cranmer and Rezende say makes their problems tractable.
