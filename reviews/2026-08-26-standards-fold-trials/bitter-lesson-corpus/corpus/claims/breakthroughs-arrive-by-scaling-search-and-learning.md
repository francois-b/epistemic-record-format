Breakthrough progress eventually arrives by an opposing approach based on scaling computation through search and learning.

## Working notes

The AlexNet authors close their paper saying results can be improved simply by waiting for faster GPUs and bigger datasets, which is the essay's thesis stated by practitioners seven years earlier. Thompson and co-authors then measure the dependency and find it real across many applications. The against side is that some breakthroughs are architectural: ResNet's authors attribute theirs to an explicit reformulation of the layers, not to scale.
