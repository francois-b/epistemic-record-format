The statistical side of the 1970s DARPA speech programme was made of newer methods based on hidden Markov models.

## Working notes

This is the essay's clearest factual problem, and it is a dating problem. The system that met and beat every stated goal of the programme was Harpy, and Harpy was not an HMM system: it compiled hand-written knowledge sources into a single fifteen-thousand-state network and ran a beam search over it. Its authors credit Baker's Dragon for the network representation, and Dragon did not win. Wikipedia dates the CMU use of HMMs for speech to about a decade after Baum's late-1960s work, and dates HMM dominance to the 1980s.

So the statistical turn is real and the essay's mechanism is real, but it happened after the competition the sentence places it in. The survey records the search for a contrary reading.
