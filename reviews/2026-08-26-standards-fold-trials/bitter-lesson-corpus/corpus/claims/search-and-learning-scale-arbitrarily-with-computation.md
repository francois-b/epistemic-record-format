Search and learning are methods that continue to scale with increased computation even as the available computation becomes very great.

## Working notes

Recorded as a bet because nothing available settles it: the essay is making a claim about a regime nobody has been in yet. The word 'seem' in the essay's own sentence concedes as much.

Thompson and co-authors put the sharpest number against it: extrapolate the observed compute dependency and progress along current lines becomes economically, technically and environmentally unsustainable. Brooks makes the same point in watts. Neither shows the methods stop scaling; both show that the resource does.
