## Working notes

Stated as a two-item list with no third candidate considered. The against side offers one: architecture, that is, the choice of what structure to put in the model before any search or learning happens. AlphaFold's authors attribute their accuracy to exactly that, and Goyal and Bengio propose lengthening the list of built-in inductive biases rather than shortening it.
