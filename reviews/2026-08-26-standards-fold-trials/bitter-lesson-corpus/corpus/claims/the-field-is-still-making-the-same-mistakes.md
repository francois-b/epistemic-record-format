The AI field has not thoroughly learned the bitter lesson and continues to make the same kind of mistake.

## Working notes

Whether the continuing behaviour is a mistake is the whole question, and the corpus cannot settle it. Brooks reports a backlash toward built-in structure at a National Academy workshop the same month, which is evidence the behaviour continues and no evidence at all that it is wrong. AlphaFold is the case that makes this uncomfortable: a system whose authors credit built-in physical and geometric constraints for a result nobody else came close to.
