## Working notes

Two assertions travel in one sentence. The niche half is defensible. The
by-hand half is not: the Admiralty code is a published two-axis notation
used across NATO, Schema.org ClaimReview is a machine-readable
interchange vocabulary, and Scite classifies citations at scale. The
author would need to drop "by hand" or say what he means by it.

No source was retrieved for the legal-citator half of the sentence, and
the automation of citator treatment flags is the place a reader would
press hardest. Recorded as partially unbacked.
