A CRM has to be maintained by hand and decays because of it

## Working notes

The decay is real and measured, in the 22 to 70 percent a year range,
but every figure comes from a company selling the remedy. More
importantly the mechanism is wrong: the sources attribute decay to job
changes and email churn, which is the world moving under a record that
was correct when written, not to a record nobody maintained. The essay's
causal claim and the literature's causal claim are different claims.
