Enterprise wikis such as Confluence and SharePoint go stale until people either mount a big clean-up or drop them entirely

## Working notes

Better backed than the CRM claim and by a sideways route: two vendors,
Notion and Guru, have built verification with expiry and re-notification,
which is machinery you build when staleness is the default state. Guru
puts a number on it, saying manual verification reaches 8 to 12 percent
of organizational content. That number is the vendor's own and no method
is given for it.

Nothing was retrieved about Confluence or SharePoint specifically, which
are the two products the essay names.
