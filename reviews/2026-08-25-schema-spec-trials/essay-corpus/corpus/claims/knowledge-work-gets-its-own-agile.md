If LLMs make it possible to operate quickly on knowledge, knowledge work will get its own round of new or renewed methodologies, probably resembling the original Agile field

## Working notes

What would settle it: a named methodology for non-coding knowledge work,
with published practices and more than one organization using it, by
2029. Recorded here because the format's only slot for a bet's
resolution criterion is the `why` of a standing entry, which an LLM must
not write. See friction-log.md F-12.

The bet's motor is the analogy, and the analogy is in trouble: the
primary account of Agile's origin gives heavyweight process and shared
values as the cause, not iteration speed.
