The unit of evidence should be one verbatim quote from one source, small enough to support a single point, carrying its link, its retrieval metadata and enough context to be used away from the source

## Working notes

The commitment is to granularity and to self-sufficiency away from the
source. Nanopublications make the same granularity choice at the level of
an RDF assertion rather than a quotation, which is the nearest prior art
and is a difference worth the author's attention rather than a refutation.
