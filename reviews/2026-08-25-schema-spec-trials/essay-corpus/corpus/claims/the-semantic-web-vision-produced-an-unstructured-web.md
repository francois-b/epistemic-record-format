The web was meant to be semantic and what accumulated was unstructured, with the semantic vision remaining largely unrealized

## Working notes

Backed by the originators' own 2006 statement that the idea remained
largely unrealized and by a 2013 count of about four million domains
carrying markup out of roughly 250 million. Both figures are relayed
through an encyclopedia and are thirteen and twenty years old; schema.org
adoption since is not addressed and would cut the other way.
