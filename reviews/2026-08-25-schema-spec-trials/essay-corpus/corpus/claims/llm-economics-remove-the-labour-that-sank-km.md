## Working notes

The premise it leans on, that manual maintenance is what sank 1990s
knowledge management, is itself unbacked in this corpus.

What would settle it: a knowledge corpus of comparable ambition
maintained at a labour cost per record an order of magnitude below the
1990s baseline, with the baseline stated. Nobody has stated the baseline.
