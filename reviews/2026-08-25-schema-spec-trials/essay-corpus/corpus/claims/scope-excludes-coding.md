This document's subject is knowledge work outside coding: researching a topic, synthesizing sources, producing documents, and keeping order over accumulated information, with coding deliberately excluded

## Working notes

A stipulation, not a proposition about the world, which is why it is a
commitment. It matters downstream: every universal negative in the essay
is to be read as scoped to non-coding knowledge work, and several of the
counterexamples this corpus records (Cursor Blame, agentblame, argument
notations aimed at programmers) sit on or across that line. Where a
counterexample comes from coding, the claim it cuts against says so.
