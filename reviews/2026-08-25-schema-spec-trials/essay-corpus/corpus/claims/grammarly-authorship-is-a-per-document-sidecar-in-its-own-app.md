Grammarly's authorship report is a per-document sidecar living inside Grammarly's own app

## Working notes

Wrong on both counts, per Grammarly's own page. It runs inside Microsoft
Word, Google Docs and Canvas rather than only in Grammarly's app, and it
attributes at the sentence rather than at the document. The essay's
description understates its nearest competitor, which weakens the
novelty claim that follows it.
