The phrase "company brain" anthropomorphizes and specifies nothing

## Working notes

The premise the essay cites cuts against the conclusion. The Y Combinator
entry it points to names a pipeline, states what the thing is not ("not a
company-wide search or a chatbot over documents"), and gives a target
artefact (an executable skills file). The word anthropomorphizes; the
request for startups behind it is specific.

The argument survives only if it is about the word as used in the
market rather than about the source the essay cites, and the essay does
not make that distinction.
