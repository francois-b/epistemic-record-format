Recording human judgment is the newest of these practices, few people think about it, and there is next to no tooling for it

## Working notes

"Next to no tooling" does not survive: architecture decision records
are a fifteen-year-old practice with a founding text, command-line tools,
a GitHub organization, format variants, and an explicit extension to any
decision record.

"Newest practice" does not survive either, on the same evidence.

What survives is narrower and is the thing worth saying: an ADR records
a decision once, in prose, and nothing revisits it. A dated, per-person,
withdrawable position on a claim was found nowhere.
