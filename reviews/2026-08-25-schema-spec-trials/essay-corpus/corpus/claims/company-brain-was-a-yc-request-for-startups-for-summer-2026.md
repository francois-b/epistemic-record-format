"Company Brain" was a Y Combinator request for startups in the Summer 2026 batch, written by Tom Blomfield

## Working notes

Checks out exactly, and only through the Internet Archive: the live
ycombinator.com/rfs page now carries the Fall 2026 list and the word
brain does not appear on it. Three months from publication to an
unverifiable primary source is worth the author knowing.

The essay's two companion assertions in the same sentence, that a cluster
of startups use the name and that the business press is pitching
"enterprise brain", were not backed by any source in this corpus.
