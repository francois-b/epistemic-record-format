Today's tools do not record whether a piece of work was written by a human or by an AI tool

## Working notes

False as a universal, and the essay refutes it itself four sentences
later by naming Cursor Blame. The corpus adds three more: agentblame,
Grammarly Authorship at sentence grain, and the Co-authored-by trailer
that AI coding tools already use.

What survives, and it is what the essay actually needs: no such record
spans a working corpus across tools. Every instance is confined to one
editor, one format or one vendor's app.
