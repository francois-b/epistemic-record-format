In company-brain products, citations point at a page or highlight a passage on screen, and where a quote is kept nothing checks it

## Working notes

The mechanism to check a citation is available to every one of these
products as a cloud API and at least one product category (research
tools) ships it. Whether any given company-brain product calls it was not
established, so the claim is neither backed nor refuted here: what is
established is that "nothing checks it" cannot mean "nothing can".
