Loose AI-flavoured prose, recycled from call to call, degrades quickly

## Working notes

The nearest measured phenomenon is convergence in LLM-driven mutation
chains: 93 percent of mutations revisiting a previously seen structural
form in 87 percent of chains, and a classical operator not converging
comparably. That is the essay's mechanism, measured on programs in a
domain-specific language, without selection pressure, in a preprint.

Model collapse is filed against the claim rather than for it because it
is the study most likely to be mistaken for support and concerns a
different mechanism: recursive training, not reuse between calls.

For prose in a working corpus with an operator in the loop, nothing was
found either way. "Degrades quickly" has no measurement behind it.
