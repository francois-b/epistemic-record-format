There is no lint for provenance: no automated check that a document's citations exist, say what they are said to say, and are themselves sound

## Working notes

False as written, and falsified by a tool that uses the essay's own
word: sciwrite-lint applies the linting paradigm to citation
verification, checks reference existence, metadata accuracy, retraction
status and claim support, runs locally, and is fast enough to re-run
between revisions. It is a 2026 preprint rather than an established tool,
which is the strongest thing that can be said in the essay's defence.
