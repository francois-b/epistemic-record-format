Knowledge engineering of the 1980s came at knowledge from the formal side and its descendant survives in Palantir's hand-built ontologies, which work for operational data

## Working notes

The Palantir half is corroborated by Palantir's own documentation: the
Ontology is an operational layer, defined by mapping data sources into
objects, properties and links. The word "hand-built" is not settled by
that documentation, which says the mapping happens without saying who or
what performs it. The 1980s lineage claim is backed by nothing here.
