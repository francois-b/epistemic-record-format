Each claim should carry a standing that only the operator moves, with every move dated in the file's history so that a withdrawal is a recorded event rather than a deletion

## Working notes

This is the one of the essay's three named properties for which the
opposite search found nothing at all. Notion and Guru mark whole pages
and let them lapse; Kialo records ratings without dating them per person;
ADRs record a decision once and never revisit it. A dated, per-person,
withdrawable position on a claim was not found in any system this corpus
reached.
