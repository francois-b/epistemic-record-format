## Working notes

No Google source was retrieved, so the claim rests on the essay alone
and is recorded as unbacked. What was retrieved cuts against it from the
side: Grammarly Authorship runs inside Google Docs and attributes at the
sentence, so the platform does not prevent finer attribution even if
Google does not provide it. Google Docs' own revision history and
activity log were not examined.
