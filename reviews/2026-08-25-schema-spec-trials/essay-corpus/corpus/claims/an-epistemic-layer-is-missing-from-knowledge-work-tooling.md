Knowledge work in the LLM era needs a layer that tracks provenance, checking and standing, and that layer is not present in mainstream tooling

## Working notes

The document's thesis. Three of its four premises are false as the essay
states them and true in a narrower form; the argument survives the
narrowing, because "not present in mainstream tooling" is weaker than
"nobody has built it" and is what the premises actually support.

The honest restatement: these primitives exist, in scholarly publishing,
in fact-checking, in RAG evaluation and in media provenance, and none of
them has reached general knowledge work. That is a claim about adoption
rather than about invention, and it is a better claim.
