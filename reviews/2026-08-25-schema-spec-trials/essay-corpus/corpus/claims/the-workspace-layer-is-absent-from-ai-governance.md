## Working notes

The one universal negative in the essay that survived its opposite
search. Nine results, none addressing the operator's own file tree as the
governed object. The two substantial instruments retrieved, Microsoft
Purview and the NIST framework, both sit in the operating and data layers
and are therefore consistent with the claim rather than against it; they
are filed as atoms_against because a reader could reasonably say they are
what an AI-governance conversation about the workspace would look like,
and the claim should have to face them.

The survey's own coverage bound is the honest caveat: the concept has no
settled name, so a single phrasing is the weakest instrument for an
absence.
