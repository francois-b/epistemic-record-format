None of the available memory, context and knowledge-graph tools adds a primitive for checked provenance, for checking claims, or for recording what a person stands behind

## Working notes

This is the essay's sharpest sentence and it does not survive. Two of the
three primitives are present in the wild and have been for over a decade.
Checked provenance: nanopublications from 2012, SEPIO, W3C PROV,
sciwrite-lint. Claim checking: Schema.org ClaimReview, Scite, Google's
check grounding API. Recording what a person stands behind: nothing found
anywhere, and the nearest relatives (ADRs, Notion and Guru page
verification, Kialo ratings) all fail on one of dated, per-person, or
withdrawable.

The defensible version of the sentence is one third as long and much
stronger: nothing records what a person stands behind, claim by claim,
with withdrawal as an event. Three surveys back the reading and their
coverage bounds are stated in each; none of them is a systematic review,
so the absence half rests on thin ground even where it holds.
