## Working notes

Nothing public supports the word "ubiquitous". The only population-level
number retrievable is about AI use generally, not dictation. A trade
publication writing on the subject in 2026 was still asking whether the
technology was good enough for common office use, which is not the
language used about something ubiquitous. The class of tool is real and
growing; the quantifier is the author's own.
