Faster iteration on software is what prompted the new methodologies that became Agile

## Working notes

The primary account gives a different cause. Highsmith, who was in the
room, says the participants convened out of a shared need for an
alternative to documentation-driven, heavyweight processes, and locates
the deeper theme in a set of values about people and collaboration.
Nothing in the account attributes the movement to iteration having become
faster.

This matters beyond the history: the essay's forward bet is that LLMs
making knowledge work faster will produce an Agile-shaped methodological
wave. If speed was not what produced Agile, the analogy loses its motor.
