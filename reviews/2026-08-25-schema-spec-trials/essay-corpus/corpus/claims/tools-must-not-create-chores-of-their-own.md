Knowledge-work tools should bring the material a task needs in the form the operator needs it, and should never create chores of their own such as correcting the AI's mistakes or re-checking what has already been verified

## Working notes

Stated as a design constraint the author holds himself to. It is in
tension with the essay's own governance finding, which is that the value
came from adding constraints, and constraints are chores until they pay.
