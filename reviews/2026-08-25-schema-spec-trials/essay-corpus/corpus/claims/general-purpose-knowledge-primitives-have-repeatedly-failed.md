Primitives for knowledge work in general have failed repeatedly

## Working notes

Two instances retrieved: Project Xanadu, which Wired called the
longest-running vaporware story in the industry, and the Semantic Web,
whose own originators said in 2006 that the idea remained largely
unrealized. Two instances are not a pattern; they are the two everyone
already names. A reader could fairly ask which general-purpose primitives
succeeded and whether the essay's sample is chosen by its conclusion.
