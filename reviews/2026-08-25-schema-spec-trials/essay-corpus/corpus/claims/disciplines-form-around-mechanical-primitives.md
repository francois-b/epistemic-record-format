A working discipline forms only once its primitives are mechanical, which is why code review formed around the diff and continuous integration around tests, and why no comparable discipline has crystallized in knowledge work

## Working notes

The essay's central argument. Granting the premises the conclusion
follows, and the premises are in trouble: one is unbacked (code review
and the diff), and the third is decomposed into three parts of which two
are false as written.

The argument also has a form problem the corpus cannot settle: two
confirming instances from one field, both selected after the conclusion
was formed. A reader who wanted to attack this would ask for a
discipline that formed without a mechanical primitive, and would find
several outside software.
