What was sought: evidence against the essay's claim that the workspace
layer of AI governance, the working environment itself treated as governed
data, is missing from the AI-governance conversation.

What was found: nothing that answers it. The governance material the search
returns is about models, risk classification, data classification,
retention and audit. Document lifecycle management is about a document's
phases under organizational policy, not about the shape an LLM is permitted
to give a new artefact. No result treated the operator's own folder
structure, document classes and lifecycle states as the governed object.

This is the one universal negative in the essay that survived its opposite
search, and it is worth saying plainly because the others did not.

Coverage bounds: one act, and a single phrasing of a concept that has no
settled name, which is the weakest possible instrument for an absence
reading. The concept's namelessness is part of the essay's point and is
also exactly what makes this search unable to find it if it exists under
another name. Treat the absence as suggestive and not as established.
