What was sought: a measurement supporting or refuting the essay's statement
that speech-to-text became ubiquitous in 2026.

What was found: no independent measurement of speech-to-text adoption among
knowledge workers. The retrievable numbers are vendor marketing and a
market-size projection. The one piece of trade press retrieved treats
mainstream office adoption as an open question in 2026.

This is a sparseness reading rather than an absence reading: the figure may
exist behind an analyst paywall or in a survey this instrument does not
index. What can be said is that the essay's word ubiquitous has nothing
public standing behind it, and that a 2026 trade publication writing on the
same subject used markedly weaker language.

paywalled sources, no vendor telemetry. Hits inspected one page deep.
