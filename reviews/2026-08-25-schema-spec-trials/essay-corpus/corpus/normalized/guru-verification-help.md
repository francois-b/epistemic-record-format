# What is Verification?

Verification is Guru's system for marking content as trusted and current. It helps your team know which information they can rely on—and helps your AI tools ground answers in accurate knowledge.

##

What is Verification?

[](#what-is-verification)

Verification is a trust signal that tells users whether content has been reviewed and confirmed as accurate. Every piece of content in Guru (Cards & connected sources) can have one of three verification states:

- **Verified** (green checkmark): Content has been reviewed and confirmed as accurate and current
- **Unverified** (gray indicator): Content has been flagged as potentially outdated or inaccurate
- **None** (no badge): Content hasn't been reviewed yet—neutral state with no indicator shown

You'll see these verification badges throughout Guru: in search results, in AI-generated answers, on Cards, and next to source documents.

##

Why Verification Matters

[](#why-verification-matters)

Most organizations face a fundamental knowledge quality problem: content decays faster than teams can review it. Manual verification processes typically reach only 8-12% of organizational content, leaving the rest to slowly become outdated.

The stakes are high. When AI gets inaccurate information, it confidently distributes that misinformation at scale. What previously affected one employee-customer interaction can affect thousands.

**The cost of wrong information:**

- Compliance violations from outdated policies
- Customer churn from incorrect support answers
- Productivity loss from employees following old procedures
- Erosion of trust in your knowledge systems

Verification creates accountability for content quality without creating bottlenecks.

##

Two Approaches to Verification

[](#two-approaches-to-verification)

**Manual Verification**: Designated people directly review and approve content, setting expiration dates or marking content as evergreen. Ideal for critical content that requires expert judgment.
