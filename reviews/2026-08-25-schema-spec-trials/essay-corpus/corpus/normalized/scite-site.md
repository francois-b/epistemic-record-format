## What sets Scite apart?

-
  ### Search content other tools can't reach

  Scite is built on the largest licensed database of scholarly content in AI research, with agreements across 40+ publishers plus the full open access corpus. When other tools hit a paywall, they guess. Scite reads the paper.
-
  ### See whether findings hold up

  Scite helps researchers move beyond finding papers to understanding whether the evidence behind them is strong, contested, or uncertain. Smart Citations show how later research has supported, challenged, or discussed a paper, claim, method, journal, author, institution, or funder, while Scite’s AI Assistant uses those same evidence signals to ground answers in the broader literature.
-
  ### More than articles

  Research doesn't live only in journals. Scite's database extends to patents, clinical trials, grants, preprints, and datasets, so you can trace an idea from funded proposal to published finding to commercial application.
