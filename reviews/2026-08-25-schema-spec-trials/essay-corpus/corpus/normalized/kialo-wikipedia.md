**Kialo** is an online structured debate platform with [argument maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_map "Argument map") in the form of debate trees. It is a collaborative reasoning tool for thoughtful discussion, understanding different points of view, and [collaborative decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_decision-making "Collaborative decision-making"), showing [arguments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument "Argument") for and against claims underneath user-submitted theses or questions.^([\[2\]](#cite_note-chonicEd-2)[\[3\]](#cite_note-fitimes-3)[\[4\]](#cite_note-about-4)[\[5\]](#cite_note-10.1145/3485447.3512144-5))

The deliberative discourse platform is designed to present hundreds of supporting or opposing arguments in a dynamic argument [tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree_structure "Tree structure")^([\[8\]](#cite_note-8)) and is streamlined for [rational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational "Rational") civil debate on topics such as [philosophical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy "Philosophy") questions, policy deliberations, entertainment, ethics, science questions, and [unsolved problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_unsolved_problems "Lists of unsolved problems") or subjects of disagreement in general.^([\[3\]](#cite_note-fitimes-3)[\[2\]](#cite_note-chonicEd-2)[\[9\]](#cite_note-makeuseofKialo-9)[\[10\]](#cite_note-Skitalinskaya-10))

Argument-boxes are structured into hierarchical branches where the root is the main thesis (or theses) of the debate, enabling [deliberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deliberation "Deliberation") and navigable [debates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debate "Debate") between opposing perspectives. A debate is divided into Pro (supporting) and Con (refuting or devaluing) columns where registered users can add arguments and [rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_system "Voting system") the impact on the weight or validity of the parent claim. The arguments are sorted according to the rating average.^([\[15\]](#cite_note-impactRatings-15))

Its argument tree structure enables detailed scrutiny of claims at all levels of the tree^([\[16\]](#cite_note-structured-16)) and allows users to for example quickly understand why a decision was made or which of the aggregated arguments swayed it this way.^([\[3\]](#cite_note-fitimes-3)) Newcomers can join a debate at any time and look back at the structured discussion history, and then weigh in at the right place with their new argument or their comment on a specific argument.^([\[2\]](#cite_note-chonicEd-2)[\[11\]](#cite_note-techlearning-11)[\[17\]](#cite_note-Mahoney-17)) The design presets a structure on debates "that allows participants to easily see, process, and ultimately assess the many facets of competing claims".^([\[16\]](#cite_note-structured-16))

The word Kialo is [Esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto "Esperanto") for "reason".^([\[3\]](#cite_note-fitimes-3)[\[2\]](#cite_note-chonicEd-2)) The platform is the world's largest argument mapping and structured debate site.^([\[18\]](#cite_note-fakenews-18)[\[19\]](#cite_note-10.1145/3461564.3461584-19))
