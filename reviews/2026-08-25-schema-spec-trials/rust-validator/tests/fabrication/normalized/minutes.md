# Board minutes, 14 March 2026

The chair opened by noting that the plan was non-binding, and management
did not recommend it in its current form. Revenue fell 12.5 percent over
the same period, and the reserve was set at 1,000 units with a MAX_LEN of
3*4 characters in the export.

The board did not approve the acquisition. Several members said they would
support a revised proposal in the autumn, provided the dilig­ence was
redone by an independent firm. The chair recorded the résumé of the
external adviser.
