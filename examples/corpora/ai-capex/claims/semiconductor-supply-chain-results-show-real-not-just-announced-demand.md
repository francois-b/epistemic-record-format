## Working notes

Record 2026 quarterly results and forward guidance across TSMC, ASML, Samsung, and SK hynix indicate AI-driven demand is materializing in upstream semiconductor revenue, profit, and capacity expansion, not only in hyperscaler capex announcements.

TSMC's Q2 2026 revenue rose 33.7% YoY with 67.7% gross margin and Q3 guidance further up (acx-88, acx-89); ASML raised full-year 2026 sales guidance to EUR43-45B and is adding 30% to both its low-NA EUV and DUV immersion capacity for 2027 (acx-90, acx-91, acx-131); Samsung's Device Solutions division posted a record quarter on AI server memory demand despite limited capacity (acx-92), with the memory market expected to stay undersupplied through H2 2026 (acx-127); SK hynix posted an all-time-high quarter (76% operating margin, acx-93), crossed 100 trillion won in H1 2026 revenue for the first time (acx-135), and began HBM4 mass shipments while still emphasizing capex discipline even as it expands (acx-94). This is upstream evidence -- money actually landing in chipmakers' and equipment makers' income statements, several links removed from any single hyperscaler's capex line -- which is why it is treated as a distinct claim from the hyperscaler-level demand-signal claims rather than folded into them. atoms_against carries Doomberg's China-competition argument (acx-53, low source_quality) not because it disputes that current results are strong, but because it names a forward-looking mechanism -- Chinese subsidized supply flooding global markets at below-competitive prices -- that could erode the very pricing power these record margins currently reflect; flagged in claimsA-friction.md as a different kind of 'against' (future competitive risk, not present-tense dispute of the facts).
