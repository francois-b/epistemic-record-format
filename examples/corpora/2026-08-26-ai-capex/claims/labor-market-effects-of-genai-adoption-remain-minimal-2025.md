## Working notes

Rigorous administrative-data studies of generative-AI chatbot adoption in exposed occupations find precisely null effects on earnings and hours worked as of 2025, despite modest individual-level time savings.

Sourced entirely from Humlum and Vestergaard's Danish administrative-labor-data study across 11 exposed occupations (acx-58: confidence intervals ruling out effects above 1% on earnings/hours, despite ~3% time-savings). This is the strongest single labor-market atom in the corpus by source_quality (high: primary NBER working paper on administrative, not self-reported, data) but it is a single study of one country's labor market; no other atom in the corpus independently replicates or contests the finding, so the claim's generalizability beyond Denmark's exposed-occupation sample is untested within this corpus -- flagged in claimsA-friction.md rather than papered over with a forced atoms_against.
