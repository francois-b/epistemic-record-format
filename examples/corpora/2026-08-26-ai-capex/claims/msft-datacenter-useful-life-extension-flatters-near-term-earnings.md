## Working notes

Microsoft's extension of the estimated useful life of datacenter and office assets from 15 to 25 years, effective FY2027, slows depreciation recognition and raises reported operating income even as cash capital expenditure continues to climb.

acx-4 states the mechanics and the stated capex consequence (CY2026 capex guidance rises to ~$175B partly because more future datacenter leases shift from finance leases to operating leases). acx-59 gives the trend this sits inside: D&A rose from $20.958B (FY2024->FY2025 step) to $29.433B to $38.534B (FY2025->FY2026), an accelerating pace even before the useful-life extension's full effect lands in FY2027. No atoms in the corpus dispute the mechanical fact that a longer assumed useful life lowers each period's depreciation charge, holding other things equal; that arithmetic is not in dispute. What is in dispute -- whether the 25-year assumption is a defensible read of AI-hardware and datacenter longevity or an aggressive one -- is a separate, harder question this claim deliberately does not adjudicate; see ai-gpu-assets-may-depreciate-faster-than-industry-schedules-assume for the argument that takes a position on it.
