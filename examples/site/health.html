Verdicts older than what they judged

Flags, not violations. ERF-47: staleness is computed and never stored. A finding_audit judged its atom, an evidence_audit judged the claim and the atoms attached to it, so an atom edited or attached after the audit makes it stale. Where two stamps differ in precision and cannot be ordered, the comparison resolves to stale: a check that cannot tell says look, never rest.
