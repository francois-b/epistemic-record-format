The major legal citators disagree substantially on identifying negative
treatment, and the leading vendor defense is that no objectively
correct interpretation exists. In the one peer-reviewed comparison
(Hellyer 2018, 357 citing relationships), all three citators agreed
negative treatment existed in only 15% of cases and agreed on its type
11% of the time; on clearly negative references, Shepard's missed 33%,
KeyCite 38%, and BCite 72%; shown the draft findings, Thomson Reuters
argued that citation analysis "does not lend itself to an 'objectively
correct' interpretation."
